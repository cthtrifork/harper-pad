What was wrong

Found by reading the styles in the editor, drawer, updates and problems panel.

Five different roles shared one look: 32px high, 1px --line border, --raised fill, 6px radius. A toolbar action, a panel toggle, a drawer opener, a fix suggestion and a close button cannot be told apart, and "on" is only a border colour change.

Button types

Pick by what the control does, then take the look from the row. Every button is a real <button type="button">.

PrimaryProposed
  • The one thing this surface is for. At most one per view or popover.
  • Accent fill, --on-accent text, weight 600, no border. 32px in toolbars, 30px in drawers and popovers.
  • Label is a verb: "New draft", "Restart to update".
SecondaryIn the app
  • A normal action that runs once and is not the main one.
  • Raised fill, 1px --line border, text colour. Keep this look for actions only.
  • A split button (Save + caret) shares one border; the caret opens a menu.
ToggleIn the app
  • Shows or hides a panel and stays that way. Set aria-pressed or aria-expanded.
  • On: --selected fill plus an accent border, so state shows by fill and not by border colour alone.
  • Counts are a small pill inside the button.
SegmentedProposed
  • Pick exactly one of 2 to 4 views. Edit, Split, Preview is the example.
  • Sunken track, the chosen segment is raised with weight 600. Never combine with Toggle styling.
Quiet and iconIn the app
  • Chrome that should not compete with the document: close, more, the idle Updates button.
  • Transparent and muted at rest; border and fill appear on hover. Icon-only buttons are 32 × 32 (30 in drawers) and need an aria-label.
DestructiveProposed
  • Removes something. --bad text; border turns --bad on hover. Never filled red at rest.
  • Do not ask "Are you sure?". Delete at once and show a toast with Undo, as the drafts list does. Drafts are kept, so say so.
Suggestion chipIn the app
teh →
  • Applies a Harper fix to the text. 24px, pill shape, soft accent tint, so it reads as a suggestion and not a command.
  • Shows the replacement text as the label, not "Fix".
Text linkIn the app
  • Secondary actions inside a sentence, toast or popover: Show more, What is new. Do not add a "Later" or "Dismiss" link to a popover: Esc, an outside click and the trigger already close it.
  • .strong (accent, 600) for the one action in a toast, such as Undo.

Focus: 2px --accent outline, 2px offset, on :focus-visible. Disabled keeps the label and uses cursor: not-allowed. A busy button keeps its width and swaps the icon for a spinner.

Menus and popovers

Menus open from a button with a caret. Popovers carry a short message and one primary action.

Menu

Rules

  • role="menu", aria-haspopup, aria-expanded on the trigger.
  • Item 30px, 8px radius on the panel, 5px on items, hover uses --selected.
  • Shortcut right-aligned in --mute, 12px.
  • Opens downward; the footer format menu opens upward (.menu.up).
  • Closes on Esc, outside click and after choosing.
  • Enter: fade and 4px slide, 0.12s.
  • A popover has one job and no dismiss link: Esc, an outside click and the trigger already close it. The Updates button keeps its text (the version) and shows state with an accent icon: a dot when an update is found or ready, a spinner while checking or downloading. The popover holds the one primary action at the bottom right and always has a "What is new" link at the bottom left.

Rules

  • Animate opacity, transform, colour. Not width or height.
  • Nothing loops except a busy spinner.
  • The prefers-reduced-motion rule in base.css turns all of it off.

UX rules

How Harper Pad behaves, so new features feel the same.

DoKeep the document centre stage. Chrome is muted until hovered.
DoSave drafts automatically and say what happened in a toast: "Saved to file".
DoOffer Undo after a delete or a fix instead of a confirm dialog.
DoShow keyboard shortcuts in menus: Ctrl+S, Ctrl+Shift+S.
DoWrite errors as what happened and what to do: "The database could not be read. Start fresh keeps the old one."
DoShow an empty state that says what appears here and how to add the first item.
Don'tUse two primary buttons in one view.
Don'tShow state by border colour alone, or by colour alone.
Don'tMake a toolbar of identical bordered buttons. Group by role and separate groups with a 1px divider.
Don'tInterrupt writing with modals. Problems, updates and drafts live in panels and popovers.
Don'tUse div or span with click handlers. Tab skips them.
Don'tUse "Click here", "OK" or "Submit". Name the action.

Before you ship UI

  • Which button type is it? The look matches the table above.
  • Colours come from tokens and read in both light and dark.
  • Hover, pressed, focus-visible, disabled and busy are all handled.
  • Icon-only buttons have an aria-label; toggles set aria-pressed or aria-expanded.
  • Everything works from the keyboard, and Esc closes menus and popovers.
  • Menus, drawers and panels animate in 0.12–0.25s with --ease, and stop under reduced motion.
  • Compared with the "Harper Pad UI" design canvas boards: Edit, Split, Preview, Scratch pad, Updates.
